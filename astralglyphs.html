How to Play Astral Glyphs

Guess the secret sequence of 7 colored orbs in 6 tries.

  • Select Color: Tap ANY orb in the top palette to select its color. Swipe the palette left/right to bring different colors closer.
  • Place Orb: Tap an empty hexagonal slot (or the center slot) on the current row to place the selected color.
  • Remove Orb: Tap an already placed orb on the current row to remove it.
  • Tilt Board: Swipe up/down on the bottom half of the screen to tilt the current guess row. (Rotation is disabled).
  • Submit: Once all 7 slots are filled, the "Submit Guess" button appears. Tap it to check your guess.

Feedback:

  • Green Slot: Correct color in the correct position. A glyph appears and the orb **glows with inner energy**!
  • Yellow Slot: Correct color but in the wrong position.
  • Gray Slot: Color not in the sequence.

Game Modes:

  • Daily Glyphs: Everyone gets the same puzzle each day! Use the Back button to return to the menu.
  • Astral Trials: A new random puzzle every time you play. Use 'New Sequence' for a fresh puzzle or 'Back' to return to the menu.
